Two choices behind this list

Phases 9 and 10 go past the roadmap, which ends with the Android app in Phase 8. The plan only lists those topics as “later”. They are kept here as optional. Also, this phone has no efficiency cores (Phase 1), so where the outline says prime/performance/efficiency, the pages compare prime with performance cores and say how other chips differ.
