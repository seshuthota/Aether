The five concepts, in order

1 through 3 get a binary executing on the Snapdragon. 4 and 5 are about thinking in time and in bytes. The decode ceiling is the idea those two are building toward, so it has its own page even though it was taught inside concept 5.

  1. Cross-compilation — host versus target, the triple, Bionic versus glibc, API level 31.
  2. The phone — adb, /data/local/tmp, and what cpuinfo actually said.
  3. Cargo and the runner — profiles, the test executable, scripts/adb-run.sh line by line.
  4. Measuring time — Instant, black_box, warm-up, median, the harness.
  5. Tensors — shape, row-major strides, the Tensor invariants.
  6. The decode ceiling — bytes per weight, and why unlimited compute does not help.

Numbers worth keeping

45 nsphone, one Instant::now measured
4.94 msmedian, 4M f32 sum measured
3.3 GB/sthat sum, not DRAM calculated
64 MiB4096×4096 f32 calculated

The 3.3 GB/s figure is 16×106 bytes in about 4.9 ms. It is far below what DRAM can do. The addition chain is waiting on itself. That distinction is the whole point of measuring time and of the roofline reading.

What was deliberately left alone

  • No dtype enum. f32 only, until quantization needs more.
  • No aligned allocator. Vec<f32> is 4-byte aligned. Phase 1 replaces that.
  • No batch timer. The harness times one call. Fine while a call is milliseconds; wrong when a call is tens of nanoseconds.
  • No views or transpose. Strides are stored so a later transpose can swap them without moving bytes. That code does not exist yet.
  • Doc-tests still run on the host. Cargo limitation. We will not write doc-tests that need the phone.

Phase 0 numbers were measured on the iQOO 13 (SM8750) in October 2026 unless tagged otherwise. Phase 1 pages were written before the measurements; the measured numbers are on What Phase 1 measured.
