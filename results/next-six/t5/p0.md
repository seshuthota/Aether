## The B7 profile was wrong

`phase-lfm3-devices.md` reported LFM3's decode shaders summing to
2.10 ms/token of a ~34 ms step, "~32 ms CPU submit/fence/wait overhead".
`profile_lfm3` read the timestamps once, after the last of 16 steps (they
hold the last submission only), and divided one step's time by 16:
2.10 × 16 = 33.6 ms, the whole step. Fixed (it now reads them after every
step). The decode step is GPU time; the submit/fence cost is under 1 ms.

## Biggest costs: the products, and attention at depth

Bandwidth by stage is the tensor bytes from the GGUF divided by the stage
time at depth 0. The GPU reads DRAM at about 66 GB/s (`phase-l3.md`).

| Model | gate/up | down | classifier | wo / out_proj | q/k/v |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gemma 4 Q4_0 | 584 MB, 11.74 ms, **50 GB/s** | 292 MB, 10.35 ms, **28** | 428 MB Q8_0, 8.56 ms, 50 | 74 MB, 2.76 ms, **27** | 82 MB, 2.19 ms, 38 |
| LFM3 Q4_K_M | 425 MB, 8.13 ms, 52 | 261 MB, 7.38 ms, 35 | 215 MB Q6_K, 5.30 ms, 41 | 31 MB, 0.99 ms, 31 | 40 MB, 1.30 ms, 31 |
| Qwen 0.5B Q4_0 | 118 MB, 2.84 ms, 41 | 59 MB, 1.74 ms, 34 | 77 MB, 1.73 ms, 44 | | |

- The products with few output rows (Gemma 4's down and wo: 1536 rows;
  k and v: 256) run at half the speed of the wide ones. `q4_0_gemv_t` gives
  each thread one row, so down launches only 24 workgroups of 64 threads.
- Decode attention: Gemma 4 takes 1.56 ms at depth 0, 5.57 at 512 and
  **26.04 ms at 2048**. At 2048 it reads about 90 MB of f32 K/V (7 full
  layers × 2048 × 512 × 2 × 4 B, plus 28 windowed layers of 512 × 256),
  about 2 ms of DRAM time. LFM3 takes 8.48 ms at 2048 for about 50 MB.
  `attention.comp` has a workgroup per query head, so each K/V head is read
  by all 8 of its query heads (4 for LFM3), and each thread walks one key
  row, so neighbouring threads read addresses 1–2 KB apart.

## What follows (T5 revised)

- P1 as planned (record once, one submit) is already the case: decode is
  recorded once and submitted once per token. Nothing to gain.
- P2: `attention_gqa.comp`, a workgroup per (K/V head, chunk) for the
  whole query group, with K and V through shared memory in coalesced
  slices.
- P1′: `q4_0_gemv_t` split along the blocks for matrices with few rows.
- Then: int8 decode for `GemmaGpu` (Qwen's `qx_gemv_q8` path), and the
  narrow K-quant products (LFM3).
