Plan: `aether-infer/plans/next-six.md` T5, branch `gpu-decode` (`51e0243`,
P0–P4). Raw logs: `p5/` (one per run, the conditions before and after in
each). Scripts: `p5-phone-first.sh` (the first attempt, over Wi-Fi adb, which

## Conditions

- 2026-11-26 evening. The first runs (`gemma-new-1`, `gemma-old-2`,
  `lfm3-new-2`, `lfm3-old-2`, `lfm3-f32dec-1`, `qwen2.5-0.5b-q4_0-*-1`)
  over Wi-Fi on battery; everything else over USB, **charging** (AC

  This is a step-down the CPU runs feel more than the GPU ones.

  depth. `aether bench -p 0 -n 32 -d 0,512,2048 -r 2 --cores 2-7 -dev

- Gemma 4's GPU decode at depth 512 was 8.5 tok/s before T5
  (`phase-gemma4.md`); it is 17–19 now, and 15–16 at 2048.
- The GPU is still behind the CPU on all three models: 0.55–0.65× for
  Gemma 4, 0.63–0.78× for LFM3 and 0.45–0.58× for Qwen.
- Through the CLI, Gemma 4's GPU decode is about 3 tok/s slower than
  `vk_gemma_decode`'s at depth 0 (20 against 23). Not explained here: the
  CLI adds sampling and its own timing, and the two ran at different
  times of the session.

## Why the GPU is still behind

From P0 and the numbers above:

- **Decode is GPU time.** The submit and fence are under 1 ms a token
  (P0's profiler fix), and wall − GPU is a few ms. Nothing is left to win
  on the CPU side of a GPU token.
- **The products are bandwidth-bound below the GPU's ceiling.** At P0 the
  wide matrices read at about 50 GB/s of the Adreno's about 66 GB/s.
  Gemma 4 at depth 0 still spends 36–40 ms of GPU time on a token whose
  weights are read once. P1 lifted the narrow Q4_0 matrices and P3 moved LFM3's products
  to int8 activations. What is left is the products' read rate.
- **The CPU reads the same weights faster.** Six Oryon cores at 2.4–2.75
  GHz run Gemma 4 at 34–37 tok/s and LFM3 at 46–47 from the same file, so
  for decode, which is a pure weight stream, the CPU's path to DRAM is the
  better one on this phone.
- **At depth, attention grows on both devices**, and P2 made it grow less
  on the GPU (Gemma 4 −17 % per token at 2048 against the old path).

The GPU remains useful where the CPU is busy: the image and audio
encoders and the split routes (`plans/multimodal-devices.md`). It is not
the faster decode device on this phone for these three models.
