## What it says

- With the screen awake, the CPU is the faster LM than the Adreno for
  every LFM3 request (image prompt 2x, ASR 2x, voice chat 1.4–2x), and
  the DSP is the fastest prompt for LFM3-VL.
- The split route pays for images: the vision encoder on the GPU takes
  0.4–0.5 s off the CPU's prompt (-20 %) and 0.2–0.3 s off the DSP's
  (-17 %), for about 0.27 GB more memory (the GPU's copies of the
  encoder). It does not pay for audio: LFM3-Audio's encoder is a small
  part of an 11 s clip's request, and the GPU hand-off costs more than it
  saves (+0 to +0.3 s).
- Whisper's GPU encoder helps base (0.69 against 0.75–0.80 s) and not
  tiny; the earlier 3.46 / 1.97 s were asleep.
- Best routes on this phone today: LFM3-VL on the DSP with the encoder on
  the GPU (1.4–1.6 s for a 256-token image), or on the CPU with it when
  decode speed matters (48 tok/s against the DSP's 26); LFM3-Audio ASR and
  voice chat on the CPU or the DSP, encoder on the CPU.
