# B5 microbenchmark: Q2_0 vs Q1_0 dot4 at 4096×4096

`kquant_bench-b5 --shape 4096x4096 --tokens 1,8`, iQOO 13 (I2401),
3 cooled rounds via `scripts/phone_ab.py`, median. The Q1_0 arm is
repacked to `Q1_0t` because `repack_weights()` is true on this CPU, as a
model load does; Q2_0 stays in the file layout (no repack).

Conditions: every job at the second step, **2.40/2.44 GHz**, board
36.9–37.0 °C, zone0 37–38 °C, battery 90 % on battery, all exits 0. The
top step was not reachable even after cooling.

| threads | tokens | Q1_0 µs / GB/s | Q2_0 µs / GB/s | Q2 time / Q1 | Q2 GB/s / Q1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1 | 518.6 / 4.55 | 731.5 / 6.45 | 1.41× | 1.42× |
| 1 | 8 | 1538.8 / 12.27 | 2285.6 / 16.52 | 1.49× | 1.35× |
| 6 | 1 | 147.8 / 15.96 | 205.6 / 22.95 | 1.39× | 1.44× |
| 6 | 8 | 461.1 / 40.93 | 609.4 / 61.94 | 1.32× | 1.51× |

Per 64 weights per row the decode kernels are 4 `cmtst` + 4 `sdot` for
Q1_0t and 4 `vqtbl` + 4 shift + 4 mask + 4 `sdot` for Q2_0: more ALU, but
twice the bytes, and the measured bytes/s is higher in every arm.
