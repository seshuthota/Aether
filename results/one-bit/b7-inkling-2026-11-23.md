## Verdict

**No-go for this phone.** The weights are 6.87 GB and the run needs
~7.1 GB at `-c 512`; 6.1 GB is available. The architecture is also a
new family: MoE, looped layers, relative attention without RoPE,
short convolutions, depth attention, SiTU — none of them in the engine.
IQ1_S, the only unreadable format, was implemented as isolated
groundwork (load, dequant, scalar dot, gguf-py goldens, host and phone).

## Memory

- Weights 6869 MB.
- KV per token per layer: 8 heads × 128 × 2 × 4 B = 8 KiB. Visits: 40
  sliding-window (capped at 1024) + 8 full → 201.3 MB at ctx 512,
  604.0 MB at 4096.
- Short-convolution state: 48 visits × 6656 channels × 3 lags × 4 B =
  3.8 MB.
- Scratch ~100–300 MB.
- **~7.1 GB RSS at 512**, against 6.1 GB `MemAvailable` (0.75 GB short
  before KV and scratch; the 22 GB of free swap is not usable at
  2.6 GB of traffic a token).

The publishers ask for 18 GB minimum (LM Studio) / 32 GB recommended
(AtomicChat); this phone is 16 GB.

## Engine work this model needs

Not present today: MoE (router, top-8, expert gather, per-expert
matrices), looped layer schedule with a KV cache and a conv state per
visit, rank-64 gated cross-pass messages, depth attention over residual
streams, relative-position attention with no RoPE (16 × 1024 bias table,
gathered by distance), learned sinks, q/k L2 clips, log-scaled positions,
static and dynamic short convolutions, SiTU, padded vocabulary and the
200058-token crop, output soft cap, GPT4O-style pre-tokenizer with
`ignore_merges`. B5b added two mechanisms to an existing dense
architecture; this is of the order of ten plus a MoE subsystem, so three
or four B5b-sized steps, against a new oracle build (llama.cpp master +
PR #32695 at `6196d98`; the local `d02b4c0d6` cannot load the file).

## IQ1_S (landed)

Format: 256 weights in 50 bytes (`f16 d`, `qs[32]`, `u16 qh[8]`). Per 32
weights: four 11-bit grid indices (2048 grids of −1/0/+1), a 3-bit scale
(`dl = d·(2s+1)`) and a delta sign (±1/8), value `dl·(grid + delta)`.
1.5625 bits a weight.

Code: `quant::BlockIq1S`, `dequantize_iq1_s`,
`scalar::dot_iq1_s_q8_0`, `quant/iq1_s_grid.rs` (the 2048-entry grid),
`GgmlType::IQ1_S` (19, 50 B a 256), `Weight::IQ1_S`, the loader, the
scalar dispatch. No NEON / i8mm kernel yet: the dispatch keeps IQ1_S on
the one-row scalar path.

Goldens: `tools/gen_iq1_s.py` → `testdata/quant_iq1_s/`, six cases
(1×256 … 64×1024, up to t=9) with random blocks, every scale and both
delta signs forced in the first block; gguf-py has no IQ1_S quantizer,
so its dequantizer is the reference. The test checks the row lookup bit
for bit and the dot within `1e-6·√n` of a float64 product, one token and
batched, one thread and three.
