# B8: 1-bit / ternary decode with Q8_K activations (phone, 2026-11-24)

Tree: `one-bit` at ade4a8d plus uncommitted B8 changes (build `ade4a8d04-dirty`).
One binary; `AETHER_ONEBIT_ACT=q8_0` selects the old Q8_0 decode path.
`scripts/phone_ab.py`, 3 alternated rounds, cooled to zone0 < 42 C and board
< 39.5 C (looser than B3/B5's 37 C: the room kept the board above 37 C), cap
floor 2.40 GHz, battery, screen on. `bench -dev cpu --cores 2-7 -t 6 -p 0 -n 64`.
Raw: `b8-fix2-phone-speed-2026-11-24.txt`.

Clean same-step pairs: 1.7B +38/+44 %, 8B +46/+51 %, ternary-2 4B +4/+4 %.
At the 2.40 step the 8B decodes ~21.3-21.6 tok/s (~22 GB/s of weights), still
well under Q4_0's ~40 GB/s: still compute-bound. Ternary gains little because
its 2-bit unpack (tbl + ushl + and), not the scale folding, dominates.

(64 + ~12 fold). Q2_0: 123 per 64 cols both ways (fold 24 -> ~12).

Phone kernel tests: 29 passed (q1_0/q2_0 against llama.cpp's Q8_K goldens,
`tools/gen_onebit_q8k.py`). Accuracy (PC): `b8-pc-kl-2026-11-24.txt`;
KLD vs llama.cpp rises ~3.5x on the 1.7B models (0.000954 / 0.000937), all
under the 0.001 gate. The phone uses Q8_K for decode only (prefill stays on
the Q8_0 smmla path).
