# B2 on the phone: Bonsai 1.7B Q1_0, NEON kernels

aether-infer `one-bit` `2529567` (NEON `sdot` `dot1`/`dot4`, i8mm `smmla`,
register sign expansion), iQOO 13 (I2401), cores 2–7, 6 threads, on
battery. These are **correctness runs, not benchmark rounds**: the phone
was not cooled between them and the 7.0 s below is an incidental figure,
not a speed record.

## Conditions

| | start | end |
| --- | --- | --- |
| battery | 65 % (status 3, discharging) | 64 % |
| quiet_therm (zone62) | 32.9 °C | 33.3 °C |
| zone0 | 33.4 °C (kernel test: 36.9 °C) | 55.3 °C |
| CPU caps (policy0 / policy6) | 1.79 / 1.02 GHz (just woken) | 2.92 / 3.51 GHz |

The screen was kept awake by the cargo runner's keepalive (Shift every
60 s) and by a wake-up before the KL run; no phone setting was changed.

## Correctness

- `kernels::tests::q1_0_matches_gguf_py` and
  `q1_0_table_expansion_matches_registers`: **pass on the phone**
  (`b2-phone-kernel-test-2026-11-22.txt`, 0.00 s). This is the real
  aarch64 `dot4`/`dot1`/`mmla4` dispatch, not qemu.
- wikitext-2, 2 chunks of 512, against llama.cpp's logits on the same
  file (`kl-base-llama-q1_0-wiki2.bin`): PPL(Q) 20.2117 (base 19.8878),
  **KLD 0.000264, same top 99.608 %**
  (`b2-phone-kl-2026-11-22.txt`). B1's scalar kernel was 0.000271 /
  99.608 % on the same base; the difference is f32 accumulation order.
- 510 tokens scored in **7.0 s** (B1 scalar: 222.8 s), ~32× faster end to
  end on this run. It is not a cooled benchmark and the caps were still
  climbing at the start, so it understates rather than overstates the
  kernels; pp128/tg64 against llama-bench are still owed.

## Not measured

- pp128 / tg64 tok/s against `llama-bench`.
- The register-vs-`SIGNS`-table A/B on the phone (`kquant_bench --ab`).
