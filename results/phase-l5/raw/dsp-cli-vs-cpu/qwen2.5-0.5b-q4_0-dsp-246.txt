The history of the printing press begins in the fifteenth century, when Johannes Gutenberg combined movable metal type, oil-based ink and a wooden press adapted from wine making. Within fifty years the new technology had spread across Europe, and by the sixteenth century the printing press was the dominant medium of communication. The printing press was a huge success and the printing industry grew exponentially.
