The history of the printing press begins in the fifteenth century, when Johannes Gutenberg combined movable metal type, oil-based ink and a wooden press adapted from wine making. Within fifty years the new technology had spread across Europe, and by the sixteenth century the printing press was the most widely used of all the printing methods.
