# The int8 GEMM layouts on the Adreno 830 (2026-11-14)

`bench/examples/vk_gemm.rs TOKENS gemma` on the phone, Q4_0 matrices of
Gemma 4 E2B's shapes, 39.2–39.6 °C at the start, clock caps 2.40/2.44 GHz,
one process per token count. `q8` is the tuned `q4_0_gemm_q8.comp` on the
transposed `MatQ4_0T`; `q8r` is P1's `q4_0_gemm_q8r.comp` on the row-major
`MatQ` that the decode GEMV reads (no second copy of the weights).

| shape | tokens | q8 ms | q8 GFLOP/s | q8r ms | q8r GFLOP/s | gap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| gate/up 12288×1536 | 128 | 3.25 | 1485 | 5.51 | 876 | 1.70× |
| down 1536×12288 | 128 | 3.32 | 1453 | 21.27 | 227 | 6.40× |

The row-major shader's weak cases are the short, wide matrices (rows 1536,
row stride 2–6 KB): 5–6.5× behind the transposed layout. gate/up, whose
row stride is 768 B, is 1.6–1.75× behind.

## The shared-memory staging experiment (rejected)

Staging each workgroup's 256 rows × 4 blocks through shared memory (so the
global reads are 64-byte runs along a row) was tried and made the average
worse: gate/up 1485 → 445 GFLOP/s, q/k/v 1415 → 435, wo 1468 → 434,
per-layer gate 595 → 298; only down improved (227 → 388) and still stayed
3.7× behind `q8`. The barrier and shared-memory traffic cost more than the
wider loads. The shader is unchanged (row-major, no staging).

## Reading

The transposed layout is 1.6–6.5× faster per product on this GPU, and the
gap is well over the 2× that would make a layout swap worth its risk. The
option with no extra memory is to make `MatQ4_0T` the only Q4_0 copy and
add a transposed-layout decode GEMV (its lanes can run along the row
dimension, where the T layout is contiguous); that needs a decode A/B
before it is accepted. The 1.05 GB second copy would keep the current
decode GEMV but is over the plan's 300 MB line.
